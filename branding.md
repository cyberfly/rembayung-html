# Rembayung Branding Analysis

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette
The branding uses a warm, earthy palette reflecting the natural and authentic nature of Malay cuisine.

- **Primary (Rich Browns):** Represents the earth, wood, and traditional spices.
  - Range: `#f7f6f5` (Lightest) to `#2a2420` (Darkest)
  - Core: `#5d534e`, `#4e433e`

- **Secondary (Muted Greens):** Represents nature, fresh herbs (ulam), and vitality.
  - Range: `#f1f8f1` (Lightest) to `#172517` (Darkest)
  - Core: `#619561`, `#4a774a`

- **Highlight (Gold/Brass):** Adds a touch of elegance, warmth, and premium feel.
  - Range: `#fffbeb` (Lightest) to `#452103` (Darkest)
  - Core: `#f9b913`, `#d8920b`

- **Accent (Warm Oranges/Reds):** Represents the heat of sambal and the richness of curry.
  - Core: `#FF7043`, `#E64A19`

### Typography
- **Headings:** `Lora` (Serif) - Adds a touch of tradition, elegance, and storytelling.
- **Body:** `Inter` (Sans-serif) - Ensures readability, modernity, and clean lines.

### Imagery Style
- **Interior:** Warm lighting, wooden furniture, nature-inspired decor.
- **Food:** Rich colors, textured presentation, focus on freshness and authenticity.

## Brand Voice
- **Tone:** Warm, Inviting, Authentic, Passionate.
- **Message:** "Experience the heart of Malaysian cuisine in a warm, inviting atmosphere."
- **Keywords:** Authentic, Soulful, Heritage, Fresh, Nature.

## Design Principles
1.  **Warmth:** The design should feel welcoming, like entering a home.
2.  **Authenticity:** Use genuine materials (wood, stone colors) and traditional patterns if possible.
3.  **Elegance:** A balance between rustic charm and modern sophistication.
